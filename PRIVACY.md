# RaceDock Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

RaceDock is a Windows desktop application that displays public motorsport timing information. This policy explains the data RaceDock accesses and stores.

## Personal information

RaceDock does not require an account and does not ask for or intentionally collect names, email addresses, contact information, precise location, payment information, advertising identifiers, or other personal information. RaceDock does not include advertising, analytics, or telemetry, and the RaceDock developer does not operate a server that receives application usage data.

## Data stored on your device

RaceDock stores application settings, technical logs, and crash-recovery information under `%LocalAppData%\RaceDock`. These files remain on your device unless you choose to share them. Technical logs can contain timestamps, enabled timing-source names, error details, and bounded excerpts of public timing-feed responses. They are used to operate and diagnose the application.

RaceDock also provides an optional diagnostic feed recorder. It runs only when you enable it and writes public timing-feed data to a folder you select.

You can remove RaceDock's locally stored data by deleting the `%LocalAppData%\RaceDock` folder. Uninstalling the application may not automatically remove this folder.

## Network connections and third-party services

When you enable a championship, RaceDock connects directly from your device to third-party motorsport timing services to retrieve public event and classification data. Like any network request, those services may receive your IP address and standard connection metadata. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms. RaceDock does not send those requests through a server operated by the RaceDock developer.

RaceDock can open GitHub release, support, and project pages in your default web browser. GitHub's privacy policy applies when you visit those pages.

When you open the What's new page, a build downloaded from the project releases asks GitHub for the latest published release number so it can tell you whether an update exists. That request sends no information about you beyond the IP address and connection metadata any web request carries. Installations from the Microsoft Store update through the Store and never make this request.

## Sharing and sale of information

RaceDock does not sell personal information. RaceDock does not transmit locally stored settings, logs, or diagnostic recordings to the developer or to advertising or analytics providers. If you voluntarily attach logs or diagnostic files to a support request, the information you choose to provide will be used only to investigate that request.

## Children's privacy

RaceDock is not designed to collect personal information from children and does not knowingly do so.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when RaceDock's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be published at this location with a revised effective date.
